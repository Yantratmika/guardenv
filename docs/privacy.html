Privacy policy

Short version: we don't take anything.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The tool

  • guardenv runs entirely on your computer.
  • It does not collect, transmit or store any data about you, your projects, your files or your secrets anywhere other than your own machine and your own Git repository.
  • It makes no network connections of its own. It has no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting and no update checks.
  • Your encryption key stays in ~/.guardenv/ on your computer. We never see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, no advertising.
  • No forms and no accounts, so nothing is collected from you.
  • The fonts and images are served from this site itself, with no third-party requests for them.
  • The pages are served by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which may keep ordinary server logs under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or use that information.

Third-party services you may use

Installing guardenv through Scoop or downloading from GitHub means you interact with those services, and their privacy policies apply to that interaction. Links on this site to GitHub or yantratmika.com lead to sites with their own policies.

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub or visit yantratmika.com.
